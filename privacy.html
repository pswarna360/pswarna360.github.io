Privacy policy

Application: HealProject rclone. Last updated November 5, 2026.

Google user data accessed

This application requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which permits creating and working with specific Google Drive files made available to the app. It does not request access to all Google Drive files.

Purpose and processing

With the account owner's authorization, this integration uploads, reads back, verifies, retains, and sometimes deletes app-created or otherwise authorized files. Files include encrypted Linux infrastructure recovery archives and research artifacts. Infrastructure backup archives are encrypted before Drive upload. Research files may not be encrypted unless the particular research job specifies encryption.

Storage and disclosures

Authorized files are stored in the account owner's Google Drive. The owner may also store separate encrypted copies of recovery archives in a private GitHub repository. Credentials and transfer receipts are held in owner-managed restricted local configurations. Google Drive user data is not sold or used for advertising, and is not used to train general-purpose AI models. The integration does not intentionally make Drive files public. Google and GitHub have separate data-handling and service-log policies.

Retention and control

Retention follows configured owner-controlled backup and research policies. Older app-managed files may be deleted after the required integrity and retention checks. The account owner can view and delete files in Google Drive and can revoke the app's Google access at any time, which interrupts future authorized transfers.

Security

Access uses OAuth credentials, restricted local storage, and purpose-limited permissions. Recovery archives are encrypted on the owner-managed Linux host before cloud upload. No system can guarantee absolute protection against every risk. This informational website does not request or store Google Drive tokens.
