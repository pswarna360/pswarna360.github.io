HealProject rclone

A privately operated Google Drive integration for moving authorized research outputs and encrypted Linux infrastructure recovery archives, with narrow permissions and verifiable transfers.

The project is maintained for the account owner's own infrastructure and development workflows. It does not offer public registration, commercial storage, or access to anyone else's Google Drive. This is an independent integration, not an official Google or rclone service.

File-scoped authorizationUses the drive.file Google OAuth scope. The integration works with files specifically created or made available to this application, not unrestricted contents of a user's Drive.
Encrypted recovery archivesInfrastructure recovery backups are encrypted on the operator-managed Linux system before transfer to Google Drive. Checksums are compared after upload and readback.
Research artifact transferPermitted research-generated files can be delivered to app-managed Drive locations. Research artifacts are not claimed to be encrypted unless their particular transfer workflow provides encryption.
Operator controlThe owner can change retention policies, remove app-managed files, or revoke Google OAuth authorization. Revocation stops subsequent operations until the owner authorizes them again.

How it works

The owner authorizes a dedicated OAuth client. A local automation process performs the authorized file transfers and confirms successful upload using receipts and hashes. No general-purpose public web server, user-facing login, or public data-sharing portal is operated by this site.

Security and privacy

OAuth credentials and infrastructure secrets remain in restricted owner-managed systems; they are not stored or transmitted through this website. Public website visitors are not asked to provide Google login credentials. Detailed handling, storage, sharing, retention, and revocation practices are described in the privacy policy.

Support and responsible use

This integration is for a small, authorized, noncommercial developer workflow. For questions about its handling of Google user data, contact the project operator at saipranavswarna@gmail.com.
